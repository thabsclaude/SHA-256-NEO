# Timing closure: 2026-11-18

## Scope

Routed Vivado 2026.1 (build 6511674) results for `uart_miner` on `xc7a100tcsg324-1` at a 10.000 ns clock, produced by `scripts/build_uart_miner.tcl`. Raw reports are in `reports/generated/` (git-ignored, regenerated by the script).

| EXP-002 | Shift-register schedule, pre-registered `h+K+W`, separate finalization cycle; UART/reset synchronizers and packet timeout | **+1.441** | **0 / 10,978** | 2,151 | 4,041 | 8.314 ns (digest comparator carry chain) |

For EXP-002 the hold slack (WHS) is +0.028 ns and the pulse-width slack is +4.020 ns, with no failing endpoints. Setup and hold are both met, so the script generated `uart_miner_timing_clean.bit`.

## Why EXP-001 was not enough

After the rolling schedule, the worst path ran schedule expansion → round-63 `t1` sum → final feed-forward addition in a single cycle. EXP-002 removes all three couplings: fixed shift-register taps replace indexed 16:1 multiplexers; `hkw = h + K[r] + W[r]` is registered one round early (next `h` equals current `g`); and the feed-forward addition has its own cycle. The compression now takes 65 busy cycles instead of 64. The remaining critical path is the 256-bit `digest <= target` comparison in `mining_core`.

## Functional evidence

All XSim testbenches passed on the EXP-002 RTL (compress, SHA256d header, mining core, UART loopback, and UART miner, with the miner bench extended to cover timeout and recovery). See `docs/VERIFICATION.md`.

## Limits of this evidence

- Timing is routed, post-implementation, for this device and tool version. No power or on-board measurement was taken.
- The bitstream has not been programmed and no physical UART exchange has been recorded.
- Throughput is not measured. A rough bound from the cycle count (about 3 × 65 compression cycles plus controller overhead per nonce) is on the order of 0.5 MH/s at 100 MHz; treat this as an estimate until measured on hardware.
